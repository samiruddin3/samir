RAWZAH — VISUAL STORY v2 (phone-first)

NEW INSTALL
1. Activate Elementor Free, WooCommerce and CartFlows.
2. Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin > Rawzah-Elementor-Landing.zip > Activate.
3. Pages > Rawzah Elementor > Import Visual Story v2 + All Images (if shown).
4. CartFlows Checkout step > Products: assign your real Rawzah product (BDT 899).
5. Edit that step with Elementor > choose Elementor Canvas.
   Folder > My Templates > Rawzah — Visual Story v2 + CartFlows > Insert.
   Use CartFlows Classic / One Column checkout.
6. Pages > Rawzah Elementor > enter your real WooCommerce Product ID.
   Enable the optional three-field mobile checkout preset and Save to match the preview.
   Keep this plugin active while using that preset. Enable guest checkout and COD.
7. Test your actual CartFlows checkout URL before sending ad traffic.

UPDATING THE PREVIOUS PACKAGE
Upload this ZIP in Plugins > Add New and choose Replace current with uploaded.
Then Pages > Rawzah Elementor > Import Visual Story v2 + All Images.
This creates a NEW v2 draft and saved template, preserving your previous pages.
Save a backup of your existing funnel design, then replace its old design with
Rawzah — Visual Story v2 + CartFlows using Elementor My Templates.
Keep the real WooCommerce product assigned to the Checkout step.

All 6 distinct images and Bengali fonts used by the page are bundled locally.
Price and delivery charges appear only in the actual order/checkout area.
The three people photographs are AI-generated illustrative usage scenes.
